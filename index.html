EMNLP2026

SARATHI: Decoupled Data-Free Probing & Adaptive Reconstruction for LLM Pruning

Dhruv Singh (second author)
EMNLP 2026, Main Conference ACCEPTED

Separates choosing which neurons to prune from compensating the weights that remain. A data-free NMF residual-subspace probe selects neurons, and an adaptive OBS step reconstructs each layer in O(d²) memory. Reaches 15-40% sparsity on OPT-6.7B without running out of memory and up to 2.05× throughput on A100 GPUs.

code ↗
  • A decoupled Probe-Reconstruct framework that removes calibration-domain bias and the O(Ld²) global memory bottleneck, giving stable 15-40% sparsity on OPT-6.7B.
  • The NMF Residual Subspace Probe connects pruning to Robust PCA (|W| = VH + S), with a safety bound that limits reconstruction error to O(τ√d ‖X‖F) for low-importance neurons.
  • Adaptive OBS Reconstruction from a 128-sample batch in O(d²) memory per layer, with a global budget set by Median Absolute Deviation thresholding so no single layer collapses.
  • A closed-form Bias Shift Compensation that corrects activation drift in post-LayerNorm models at zero extra cost, sharply lowering WikiText-2 perplexity after pruning.
Review2026

LILA: Calibration-Free Structured Pruning of LLMs via Latent Spectral Geometry

Dhruv Singh (second author)
Under review

Scores FFN neurons with NMF and a closed-form KS-distance between singular-value spectra, keeping the native model graph. Beats PruneNet by up to 2.12 pp and SliceGPT by up to 6.1 pp in zero-shot accuracy, and the sensitivity variant prunes a 7B model in 3.3 minutes.

  • Uses non-negative matrix factorization to score and compress FFN layers, with no PCA rotations and no RMSNorm deletions.
  • The KS-Distance Spectrum Score compares the empirical CDFs of singular values of the full and ablated matrices.
  • Per-neuron randomized SVD, justified by Weyl's perturbation inequality, brings LILA-Spectrum to 54.3-70.5 minutes on a 7B model; LILA-Sensitivity takes 3.3 minutes (about 19× faster) and matches baselines after LoRA recovery.
  • Grounded in the Neural Tangent Kernel trace ratio: LILA-Residual gives ρ = 2.79 against 47.38 for random pruning, a 17× lower functional distortion.

WIP2026

Where Does Horizontal Steering Act in Mixture-of-Experts Models? Causal Mediation of Routing Drift

Dhruv Singh (first author)
Under preparation

A four-pass causal mediation framework that splits activation-steering effects into routing, attention and expert pathways, across 10 steering methods on OLMoE and DeepSeek-V2-Lite.

  • A first-order mass-transfer identity predicts the routing effect (r = 0.83 to 0.96), showing that routing drift supports steering rather than working against it.
  • Matched logit effects do not guarantee matched shifts in generated text.
  • An 8×A100 testbed with a 260-job scheduler and 290 tests reproduces 10 official baselines.
  • A new expert-drop criterion beats every published pruning criterion on both models.
WIP2026

Stateful NMF Defense: A Sliding-Window Reconstruction and Latent-Entropy Framework against Model-Extraction Attacks

Dhruv Singh (second author)
Under preparation

Tracks reconstruction error and latent entropy over a sliding window of queries. Cuts attacker accuracy to near chance (about 10%) on CIFAR-10 / ResNet-34 while keeping 83.4% clean accuracy.

  • Tested against KnockoffNet, JBDA-TR and defense-aware attacks (D-DAE, D-DAE+, pBayes).
  • Also validated on VGG-16-BN, CIFAR-100 and CUB-200.
  • Found a failure mode when attacker and victim data overlap (Caltech-256), which motivates ongoing work on task-adaptive calibration.
